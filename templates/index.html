About This Tool

What is CancerSight?

CancerSight analyzes breast tissue histopathology images using a fine-tuned EfficientNet deep learning model to detect cancer at the cellular level.

EfficientNet Architecture

Built on EfficientNet, a state-of-the-art CNN architecture optimized for both accuracy and computational efficiency. Trained on thousands of histopathology patches.

High Clinical Accuracy

Achieves 98.36% accuracy, 0.99 precision, and 0.9978 AUC-ROC on validation data — performance comparable to experienced pathologists on image review.

Medical Images Only

This model is trained exclusively on histopathology breast tissue images. General photographs or non-medical images will produce unreliable results and will be flagged.

How This Model Works
  • Accepts histopathology breast tissue patches (JPG/PNG)
  • Image resized to 224×224 and normalized automatically
  • EfficientNet extracts deep cellular-level features
  • Outputs probability score (0–100%) for malignancy
  • Threshold at 0.5 — above: Malignant, below: Benign
Image Guidelines
  • H&E stained histopathology slides work best
  • Use high-magnification microscopy images (40×–400×)
  • Ensure image is not blurry or over-compressed
  • Do not upload selfies, X-rays, or MRI scans
Medical Disclaimer: This is an AI-based assistive system and is not a substitute for professional medical diagnosis. Always consult a qualified pathologist or oncologist for clinical decisions.
